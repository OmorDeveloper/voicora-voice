# Sayro AI — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Sayro AI ("the app") turns text into speech **on your device**. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your text and audio stay on your phone.

## What the app processes

| Data | Where it goes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Text you type, paste or import (TXT, PDF, EPUB) | Processed and stored **only on your device** | To generate speech and keep your history and projects |
| Generated audio | Stored **only on your device**, in private app storage | So you can play, export and share it |
| Chats, pronunciation rules and settings | Stored **only on your device** | To apply your preferences |
| Text sent to Sayro by other apps through Android's text-to-speech system | Processed **in memory on your device**, not stored | To speak it for that app |

The app has **no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting**. It does not collect personal information, device identifiers or location.

## Network use
The app connects to the internet for one purpose only: downloading the Sayro Voice model from Sayro's release page on GitHub (`github.com` and its download host `release-assets.githubusercontent.com`) the first time you set it up, when new voices are added, or when you repair it. That request contains no information about you or your content beyond what any web download involves (such as your IP address, which GitHub processes under its own privacy policy). Speech generation itself never uses the network.

## Sharing
Your text and audio leave your device only when **you** choose to share or export a file. You pick the destination (another app, a folder, cloud storage of your choice).

## Backups
App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your content is not copied to Google servers by the system. To move your work, export audio files or projects (`.sayro`) yourself.

## Deleting your data
- Delete individual clips or projects in the app.
- Settings → Storage & privacy → **Clear history** deletes all generated audio and text.
- Settings → Model → **Delete model** removes the AI model.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes
If a future version adds an optional online feature (for example, account sign-in or settings sync), this policy will be updated before that feature is released, and the feature will be opt-in.
