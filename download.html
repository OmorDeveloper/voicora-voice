Install in 3 steps

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser (Settings → Apps → your browser → Install unknown apps).
  3. Tap Install, open Voicora, and download the voice model once (about 104 MB, Wi-Fi recommended). After that everything works offline.

Is it safe?

The APK is built from the same code that is being reviewed for Google Play and is signed by the Voicora developer. It asks only for notification permission and needs internet only for the one-time model download. Your text and audio never leave your phone (privacy policy).
